SAT Success Tips

Time Management

Divide your time: ~13 min per passage in Reading, ~11 min per passage in Writing.

Reading Strategy

Read questions first, then the passage. Underline key points as you read.

Math Shortcuts

Plug in answer choices when stuck. Eliminate obviously wrong answers first.

Essay Approach

Spend 5-10 minutes planning. Focus on structure: intro, 2-3 body paragraphs, conclusion.
