How to Use Character Relationship Web

Getting Started

  • Add Characters: Click "Add Character" in the panel or double-click on the workspace
  • Move Characters: Drag and drop character nodes anywhere on the workspace
  • Connect Characters: Select two characters and click "Connect" or drag from one character to another
  • Edit Relationships: Click on relationship lines to edit type, intensity, and history

Workspace Navigation

  • Zoom: Use the zoom buttons or mouse wheel to zoom in/out
  • Pan: Click and drag on empty workspace area to pan
  • Minimap: Use the minimap in the bottom-right to navigate large workspaces
  • Scroll: Scroll vertically and horizontally to explore large relationship maps

Advanced Features

  • AI Insights: Get automatic relationship suggestions based on character positions
  • Timeline: Use the timeline slider to visualize how relationships change over time
  • Search: Quickly find characters using the search bar
  • Filter: Filter relationships by type using the tags
  • Export: Export your relationship map as PNG, SVG, JSON, or PDF
  • Auto Layout: Automatically arrange characters for better visualization
Tips & Tricks

• Characters placed closer together suggest stronger relationships

• Use different colors to represent different factions or groups

• Higher intensity relationships appear with thicker lines

• Save your work regularly by exporting to JSON
