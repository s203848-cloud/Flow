How to Use These Harmonies

  • 3rd Harmony: Creates a rich, full sound and is commonly used in pop and rock music.
  • 5th Harmony: Adds power and stability, often used in choruses for emphasis.
  • Octave: Doubles the melody at a higher or lower pitch, adding depth without changing harmonic function.
