How It Works

This age calculator calculates your exact age down to the millisecond. The animated digits scroll smoothly as your age increases in real-time.

Features:

  • Enter your birth date and time (time is optional, defaults to noon)
  • Choose between local time or UTC timezone
  • Watch your age update in real-time with smooth digit animations
  • Pause the counter to examine your exact age at any moment
  • See your age broken down into years, months, days, hours, minutes, seconds, and milliseconds
  • Get birthday notifications when you reach exact year milestones

Note: The calculation accounts for leap years and varying month lengths. Milliseconds update in real-time for the most precise age measurement possible.
