AP Exam Success Tips

Time Management

MCQ section: ~1 minute per question. FRQ section: Budget time for each question. Leave time to review.

FRQ Strategy

Read all questions first. Outline your response. Use specific examples and evidence. Answer all parts of the question.

MCQ Strategy

Eliminate obviously wrong answers first. Watch for absolute language. Answer every question (no penalty for guessing).

Content Review

Focus on major themes and concepts. Practice with released exams. Review your mistakes to understand patterns.
