How to Use This Tool

  1. Upload a video - Click the upload area and select an MP4 video file from your device.
  2. Adjust frame rate - Use the slider to set how many frames per second you want to extract. Lower values (like 0.5) will give fewer frames, higher values (like 10) will give more frames.
  3. Set maximum frames - Enter the maximum number of frames you want to extract. You can enter any positive number with no upper limit.
  4. Extract frames - Click the "Extract Frames" button to process the video and generate frame images.
  5. Download frames - Once extraction is complete, click "Download All" to get a ZIP file containing all extracted frames.
Important: All processing happens in your browser. Your video is not uploaded to any server. For very large videos or high frame counts, processing may take some time.
