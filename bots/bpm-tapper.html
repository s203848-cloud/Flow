How to Use the BPM Tapper

1. Start playing a song you want to measure

2. Tap the button (or press spacebar) in time with the beat

3. After 4+ taps, your BPM will be calculated

4. Continue tapping to refine the accuracy

Common BPM Examples

  • Slow Ballads: 60-80 BPM
  • Hip-Hop/R&B: 80-110 BPM
  • Pop/Rock: 110-130 BPM
  • House/EDM: 120-130 BPM
  • Drum & Bass: 160-180 BPM
