Learning Modules

Ocean Basin Architect

Design ocean basins with tectonic plates, create seafloor features, and simulate basin formation processes. Understand plate tectonics through interactive manipulation.

Available

Currents & Climate Engine

Master ocean circulation patterns, wind-driven currents, and climate connections through interactive simulation. Explore thermohaline circulation and climate feedbacks.

Available

Marine Ecosystem Builder

Create diverse marine habitats, build food webs, and explore ecological relationships in ocean environments. Simulate population dynamics and human impacts.

Available

Coastal Geography Studio

Design coastlines, simulate erosion/deposition processes, and study land-sea interactions. Explore sea level rise impacts and coastal management strategies.

Drag & Drop Components

Build oceans by dragging tectonic plates, water masses, and ecosystems onto the interactive canvas.
