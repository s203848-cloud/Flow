GRE Success Tips

Verbal Reasoning

Learn root words and prefixes. For reading comprehension, read questions first, then the passage. Eliminate obviously wrong answers.

Quantitative Reasoning

Memorize key formulas (geometry, algebra). Use estimation when exact calculation is time-consuming. Check units and conversions.

Analytical Writing

Spend 5 minutes planning your essay structure. Use specific examples. Vary sentence structure and vocabulary.

Time Management

Skip difficult questions and return later. Use the "mark" feature to flag questions. Pace yourself to finish with time to review.
