Instructions

This calculator computes common statistical measures for a dataset:

  • Mean (Average): The sum of all values divided by the count
  • Median: The middle value when data is sorted
  • Mode: The most frequently occurring value(s)
  • Range: Difference between maximum and minimum values
  • Variance: Measure of data dispersion (sample or population)
  • Standard Deviation: Square root of variance

Note: Choose "Sample Data" if your data is a sample from a larger population, or "Population Data" if it represents the entire population.
