How It Works

This counter displays the current date and time with a scrolling digit effect. You can select any time zone from the dropdown to view the time in that specific zone. The counter updates every millisecond for maximum precision.

The World Clock section shows the current time in every time zone around the world. Use the search box to filter time zones by city, country, or region.

You can pause the counter to examine the current time, then resume to continue tracking real-time updates.
