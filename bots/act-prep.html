ACT Success Tips

English Section

Focus on grammar rules, punctuation, and sentence structure. Read the entire sentence, not just the underlined portion.

Math Section

Memorize key formulas. Use your calculator strategically. Skip difficult questions and return to them later.

Reading Section

Read the questions first, then the passage. Underline key points. Practice skimming for main ideas.

Science Section

Focus on data interpretation from graphs and tables. Don't get bogged down in scientific jargon.
