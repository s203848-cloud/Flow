How to Use

  1. Place Vertex: Click on the canvas to place the vertex (center) of the angle.
  2. Draw First Arm: Click to place the starting point of the first arm.
  3. Draw Second Arm: Click to place the end point of the second arm.
  4. Adjust: Drag any point to adjust the angle or use the controls below.
  5. Reset: Click "Reset Protractor" to start over or "Clear Lines" to remove guide lines.
