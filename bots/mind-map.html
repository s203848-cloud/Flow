How to Use

  • Start by entering your central topic in the main input field
  • Choose a color theme that suits your content
  • Click "Generate Mind Map" to create the initial structure
  • Use the "Add Nodes" section to add new ideas and details
  • Click on nodes to edit or delete them
  • Drag nodes to rearrange your mind map layout
