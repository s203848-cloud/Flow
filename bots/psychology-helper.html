Psychology Concepts

Behaviorism: Focus on observable behavior and learning through conditioning.
Cognitive Therapy: Focuses on changing negative thought patterns.
Mindfulness: Practice of being present and aware without judgment.
Resilience: Ability to adapt and recover from adversity.
Emotional Regulation: Strategies to manage and respond to emotional experiences.

Resources & Support

If you're struggling, consider reaching out to these resources:

  • Crisis Hotline: 1-800-273-8255
  • Mental Health America: mhanational.org
  • Find a Therapist: psychologytoday.com
  • Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741

If you're in immediate danger or experiencing a mental health emergency, please call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.
