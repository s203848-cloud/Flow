How It Works

This balancer uses the matrix method to solve for coefficients that satisfy the law of conservation of mass. Each element must have the same number of atoms on both sides of the equation. The coefficients are always the smallest possible whole numbers.

Instructions

1. Enter your chemical equation in the format: Reactant1 + Reactant2 → Product1 + Product2
2. Use standard chemical notation (e.g., H2O, CO2, CH4)
3. Use '+' to separate multiple reactants or products
4. Use '→' or '=' to separate reactants from products
5. Click "Balance Reaction" to get the balanced equation with coefficients
