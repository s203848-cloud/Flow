How Auto-Tune Works

Auto-tune detects the pitch of your voice and adjusts it to the nearest note in the selected musical scale. The Correction Speed controls how quickly pitch adjustments happen. Slow speeds create natural-sounding corrections, while fast speeds create the robotic "T-Pain effect".
